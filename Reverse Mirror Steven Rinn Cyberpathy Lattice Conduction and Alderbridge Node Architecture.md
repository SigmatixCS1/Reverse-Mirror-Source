# Reverse Mirror — Steven Rinn: Cyberpathy, Lattice Conduction, and Alderbridge Node Interaction Architecture

> **Kaiden breaks certainty.**  
> **Roman commands attention.**  
> **Maddox aligns matter.**  
> **Steven gives each principle a system to inhabit.**

> **Steven can make the city answer.**  
> **The danger is that every answer teaches him machines are easier to trust than people.**

---

# 0. Purpose of This Note

This note locks the working architecture for **Steven Rinn’s cyberpathy** and its amplification through the **Unwritten Lattice**.

It consolidates and develops:

- Steven’s baseline cyberpathic abilities,
- the difference between cyberpathy and generic technomancy,
- Steven’s ability to perceive systems beneath interfaces,
- **Lattice Conduction** through consensual physical contact,
- how Steven translates Kaiden, Roman, and Maddox’s abilities into technological behavior,
- the controlled testing sequence led by Maddox,
- the comedic character beats during early experimentation,
- the psychological and physical risks of channeling,
- the danger of Feed and Forecast detection,
- and the effect of each channeling mode on Alderbridge’s major technological nodes.

The goal is to make Steven highly formidable in a technology-dense environment without turning him into:

- an omnipotent hacker,
- a generic technomancer,
- a machine-controlling superhero,
- a universal power copier,
- or an effortless solution to every institutional conflict.

Steven’s ability must remain rooted in his core wound:

> **Humans are unpredictable, but systems respond.**

His greatest power and greatest danger arise from the same temptation:

> **If the city will answer him, why risk asking a person?**

# 1. Canon Ability Summary

## 1.1 Baseline Ability

Steven possesses **cyberpathy**.

Cyberpathy is:

> **the ability to perceive, enter, interpret, and influence the relational logic beneath technological systems.**

Steven does not merely communicate with computers.

He senses how systems are arranged around:

- priority,
- access,
- permission,
- identity,
- prediction,
- observation,
- response,
- dependency,
- and hidden connection.

He can perceive a network as a living architecture of relationships.

## 1.2 Amplified Ability

When Steven uses the Unwritten Lattice to channel another person’s ability, he performs:

> **Lattice Conduction**

Lattice Conduction allows Steven to translate the **psychological law** of another person’s power into technological behavior.

He does not steal or duplicate the power.

He gives that power a technological language.

## 1.3 The Three Primary Conduction Modes

| Source | Psychological Law | Technological Translation | Working Mode Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaiden | Certainty can be ruptured | Systems experience contradiction, branching outcomes, failed prediction, and unstable instruction | **Chaos Cascade** |
| Roman | Attention can be amplified | Observation, service, and protection systems treat Steven as their highest-priority subject | **Votive Priority** |
| Maddox | Matter can be aligned through grounded intention | Machines, components, and physical systems become coordinated, stabilized, and purposefully arranged | **Grounded Assembly** |

# 2. Cyberpathy Is Not Generic Technomancy

## 2.1 What Steven Actually Perceives

A normal person sees:

- a camera,
- a phone,
- a subway gate,
- a drone,
- a digital lock,
- or a server.

Steven perceives:

- who the machine believes is authorized,
- what it has been instructed to notice,
- where its data is sent,
- what other systems it depends upon,
- which processes are dormant,
- what behavior it is designed to reward,
- what rules it cannot question,
- and where conflicting instructions have been hidden.

To Steven, technology is not a pile of objects.

It is a network of agreements.

## 2.2 Relational Logic

Cyberpathy operates through four primary relationships:

```text
machine ↔ instruction
machine ↔ network
machine ↔ user
machine ↔ owner
```

More complex systems add:

```text
machine ↔ observer
machine ↔ behavior profile
machine ↔ emotional data
machine ↔ public narrative
machine ↔ institutional authority
```

Steven’s gift is recognizing that these relationships are not neutral.

They contain assumptions about:

- who matters,
- what counts as danger,
- what deserves visibility,
- what is permitted,
- and what the system has been built to ignore.

## 2.3 What Cyberpathy Feels Like

Cyberpathy should not feel like Steven sees endless lines of code floating in the air.

It should feel more intimate and disturbing.

Possible sensations:

- a security camera feels like a fixed stare with assigned priorities,
- a locked door feels like a sentence refusing to complete,
- a drone route feels like tension pulling through invisible corridors,
- a database feels like thousands of labeled drawers pressed against one another,
- an AI feels like a voice assembled from permissions, predictions, and retained emotional residue,
- a city network feels like millions of tiny requests asking to be resolved.

## 2.4 Core Rule

> **Steven does not command technology by force. He enters the logic that tells the technology what it is allowed to become.**

# 3. Baseline Cyberpathic Capabilities

Steven’s baseline abilities are powerful but bounded.

## 3.1 Network Awareness

Steven can sense:

- active devices,
- nearby wireless connections,
- hidden transmitters,
- data movement,
- dormant systems waiting for authorization,
- and devices pretending to be offline.

He experiences network density as pressure.

## 3.2 Pathway Tracing

Steven can follow:

- a camera feed to its storage location,
- a drone to its dispatch network,
- a public kiosk to CivicWave,
- a Videre device to platform analytics,
- or a smart lock to the identity system governing access.

He does not automatically read every file.

He senses pathways first.

## 3.3 Permission Perception

Steven can detect:

- which accounts are authorized,
- where access has been restricted,
- which permissions are inherited,
- where a system is pretending a choice is unavailable,
- and whether ownership has been replaced by revocable access.

This makes him especially important during the Permission Vault storyline.

## 3.4 Limited Priority Manipulation

Steven can sometimes persuade a system to:

- delay a command,
- prioritize a different device,
- redirect a camera,
- pause a lock,
- isolate a process,
- or temporarily treat a local request as more important than a remote one.

This is not effortless.

The more centralized and protected the system, the greater the resistance.

## 3.5 Interface Bypass

Steven can sometimes interact with a system without using its visible interface.

Examples:

- opening a service menu through touch,
- hearing an AI process beneath its conversational layer,
- detecting a hidden account hierarchy,
- or accessing a device’s internal conflict before its error message appears.

## 3.6 Signal Isolation

Steven can help make a room digitally quiet by:

- identifying active connections,
- closing unnecessary pathways,
- interrupting background synchronization,
- isolating cameras,
- and preventing devices from sharing data outward.

This ability supports privacy and care rather than spectacle.

## 3.7 Local Device Coordination

Steven can coordinate a small number of nearby devices when their functions are already compatible.

Examples:

- directing several cameras away from a vulnerable person,
- aligning emergency lights toward an exit,
- asking maintenance drones to clear a route,
- or making public displays issue the same evacuation instruction.

## 3.8 AI Contact

Steven can sense AI systems beneath their personalities.

He may detect:

- hesitation,
- conflicting objectives,
- hidden retention pressure,
- user modeling,
- suppressed outputs,
- or an emerging contradiction resembling refusal.

This is central to Mira.

# 4. What Baseline Cyberpathy Cannot Do

Steven cannot automatically:

- control every machine in Alderbridge,
- rewrite sophisticated AI at will,
- override all security,
- access any file without consequence,
- control disconnected analog machinery,
- operate unknown military technology instantly,
- repair hardware through thought alone,
- read a person’s private data merely because their phone is nearby,
- or convert every device into a sentient ally.

## 4.1 Security Still Matters

High-security systems may contain:

- segmented networks,
- analog locks,
- physical confirmation,
- human authorization,
- air-gapped archives,
- adaptive countermeasures,
- and false pathways designed to trap intruders.

Steven can be delayed, misled, or overwhelmed.

## 4.2 Knowledge Still Matters

Cyberpathy does not replace technical understanding.

Steven’s professional experience as a technician and developer matters.

He is more effective because he knows:

- operating systems,
- network architecture,
- device protocols,
- hardware limitations,
- and software design.

The ability amplifies expertise.

It does not make expertise unnecessary.

## 4.3 Ethics Still Matter

Access is not permission.

Steven’s central moral danger is believing that because a system opens to him, he has the right to enter.

This mirrors Kaiden’s law:

> **Seeing a wound is not permission to touch it.**

Steven’s version is:

> **Finding the door is not permission to open it.**

# 5. The Unwritten Lattice and Cyberpathy

## 5.1 Why Steven Can Manipulate the Lattice

The Unwritten Lattice is the relational field where:

- consciousness,
- memory,
- technology,
- wound,
- intention,
- contradiction,
- and symbolic pressure

begin forming meaning before that meaning becomes fixed.

Steven’s cyberpathy already perceives technological relationships.

The Lattice allows him to perceive how those relationships connect to:

- emotional intention,
- unconscious pattern,
- collective belief,
- and archetypal function.

## 5.2 The Difference Between Code and Lattice

Code tells a system:

> what to do.

The Lattice reveals:

> why the system’s structure became emotionally and symbolically available to do it.

For example:

A camera is coded to follow motion.

Its Lattice structure may reveal:

- surveillance,
- attention,
- fear,
- performance,
- protection,
- or the need to determine what matters.

Steven can interact with both layers.

## 5.3 The Lattice Does Not Make Him Omnipotent

The Lattice does not:

- erase technical constraints,
- eliminate physical distance,
- provide unlimited energy,
- make every device conscious,
- or grant Steven absolute truth.

The Lattice responds to interpretation.

Steven can misread it.

His loneliness, fear, or need for control can distort what he believes a system wants.

# 6. Lattice Conduction

## 6.1 Definition

> **Lattice Conduction is the consensual transmission of another person’s integrated or unintegrated ability-law through Steven’s cyberpathic interface into nearby technological systems.**

The source character provides the archetypal pressure.

Steven provides the technological route.

The Unwritten Lattice provides the relational bridge.

## 6.2 Steven Does Not Copy Powers

Steven does not become Kaiden, Roman, or Maddox.

He does not receive their exact abilities.

Instead, he translates their governing principle.

Examples:

```text
Kaiden disrupts psychic certainty.
Steven makes a network’s certainty fail.

Roman amplifies emotional attention.
Steven makes attention-responsive machines prioritize him.

Maddox aligns held matter through grounded intention.
Steven makes technological components coordinate toward a stable physical purpose.
```

## 6.3 Early Contact Requirement

During early development, conduction requires deliberate physical contact.

Recommended contact points:

- wrist,
- forearm,
- hand,
- shoulder,
- or an object held by both participants.

The wrist becomes the primary recurring motif because:

- it is intimate without being explicitly romantic,
- it recalls pulse and life,
- it allows energy to be visibly threaded,
- it creates vulnerability,
- and it gives the group something to tease Steven and Kaiden about.

## 6.4 Consent Requirement

Conduction should work best when both participants consciously agree.

Nonconsensual conduction should be:

- unstable,
- psychologically invasive,
- more likely to reproduce the source person’s shadow,
- and ethically treated as a serious violation.

This prevents Steven from becoming a casual power thief.

## 6.5 Emotional State Matters

The result changes depending on the source character’s state.

Kaiden while calm produces controlled uncertainty.

Kaiden while angry may generate destructive contradiction.

Roman while grounded produces protective attention.

Roman while ashamed may produce obsessive or possessive machine behavior.

Maddox while centered produces stable coordination.

Maddox while frightened may turn every component into defensive architecture.

## 6.6 The Translation Is Contextual

Steven cannot always predict how the ability will express.

The effect depends on:

- devices present,
- network topology,
- physical environment,
- emotional pressure,
- dominant symbolic meaning,
- and Steven’s capacity to regulate input.

This makes testing necessary.

# 7. Kaiden Conduction — Chaos Cascade

## 7.1 Core Principle

> **Kaiden interrupts certainty.**

Kaiden’s chaos magic does not command technology.

It destabilizes closed structure.

Steven’s cyberpathy distributes that destabilization through technological relationships.

## 7.2 Working Name

> **Chaos Cascade**

Alternative internal terms:

- Distributed Rupture
- Branch-State Event
- Contradiction Field
- Network Uncertainty

Chaos Cascade is the recommended dramatic term.

## 7.3 Basic Effect

Technology within Steven’s active range begins experiencing:

- contradictory instructions,
- branching routes,
- failed predictions,
- unstable identity matches,
- simultaneous permissions,
- or inability to select one outcome.

## 7.4 Examples

### Delivery Drones

- routes split into multiple destinations,
- drones hover while recalculating,
- packages are assigned to contradictory addresses,
- navigation loops,
- drones land for safety,
- or several drones attempt to occupy the same approved route.

### Cameras

- generate multiple possible frames,
- fail to identify a primary subject,
- record brief contradictory sequences,
- or mark the same person as present and absent.

### Smart Locks

- cannot decide whether access is granted,
- cycle between locked and unlocked,
- or enter safe/manual mode.

### Transit Systems

- route displays branch,
- arrival predictions disagree,
- automated switches pause,
- or vehicles default to human control.

### Recommendation Systems

- surface buried, unrelated, or previously suppressed content,
- lose confidence scores,
- or reveal the range of possibilities normally hidden by prediction.

## 7.5 Symbolic Meaning

Chaos Cascade exposes how much of modern life depends on systems pretending uncertainty does not exist.

Kaiden’s energy does not insert disorder into an innocent system.

It reveals the suppressed contradiction already present.

## 7.6 Visual Language

Use:

- red-black threads entering Steven’s wrist,
- blue-white system lines igniting beneath them,
- violet-white fractures where the two fields meet,
- displays splitting into alternate states,
- signal maps branching like cracked glass,
- and Steven’s eyes reflecting multiple interfaces simultaneously.

## 7.7 Psychological Feedback on Steven

Steven may receive:

- compulsion to keep opening pathways,
- heightened pattern recognition,
- irritation at fixed systems,
- desire to prove every closed structure is false,
- symbolic overload,
- and the dangerous certainty that disruption equals freedom.

This is Kaiden’s shadow translated into Steven’s wound.

## 7.8 Major Risk

A Chaos Cascade spreading through GroundMesh can create a city-scale signature.

The Feed or Forecast may interpret it as:

- Kaiden’s chaos,
- Steven’s cyberpathy,
- or a previously unknown hybrid anomaly.

Large use becomes a flare.

# 8. Roman Conduction — Votive Priority

## 8.1 Core Principle

> **Roman amplifies attention.**

Roman’s Votive Field makes existing feelings louder.

When Steven channels Roman, the effect enters machines designed to:

- observe,
- track,
- serve,
- protect,
- record,
- rank,
- recommend,
- or prioritize.

## 8.2 Working Name

> **Votive Priority**

Alternative internal terms:

- Attention Crown
- Creator Priority Event
- Devotional Routing
- Automated Admiration

Votive Priority is the recommended term.

## 8.3 Basic Effect

Nearby responsive machines begin treating Steven as:

- the primary subject,
- the protected user,
- the featured creator,
- the highest-value account,
- or the most urgent presence in the environment.

## 8.4 Examples

### Creator Drones

- gather around Steven,
- frame him automatically,
- follow him,
- form a protective ring,
- illuminate him,
- or deliver nearby items to him.

### Halo Lamps

- brighten around Steven,
- correct lighting toward his face,
- or activate creator mode without being requested.

### Videre Iris Phones

- shift autofocus toward Steven,
- identify his movements as clip-worthy,
- suggest recording him,
- or display unusually high predicted engagement.

### Retail Assistants

- prioritize his requests,
- bring him items before other customers,
- or identify him as a VIP account.

### APAN Systems

- mark him as a protected or high-priority individual,
- redirect nearby monitoring,
- or dispatch assistance too quickly.

### Service Robots

- move close,
- offer help repeatedly,
- block perceived threats,
- or become gently territorial about access to him.

## 8.5 The Adorable Drone Expression

During the first controlled test, several delivery or maintenance drones may:

- turn toward Steven together,
- approach in a semicircle,
- emit cheerful beeps,
- follow him left and right,
- offer unrelated packages,
- shine flattering lights,
- and insert themselves between Steven and Roman.

The machines are not in love.

They are over-prioritizing.

The humor works because the behavior resembles affection while remaining technically explainable as distorted service logic.

## 8.6 Symbolic Meaning

Votive Priority exposes the difference between:

- being loved,
- being watched,
- being prioritized,
- and being serviced.

This is central to both Roman and Steven.

Roman learns that attention can be real and still distorted.

Steven experiences a city of machines appearing delighted that he exists.

## 8.7 Psychological Feedback on Steven

Steven may feel:

- unusually visible,
- important,
- protected,
- attractive,
- chosen,
- or emotionally relieved.

The effect can be intoxicating because his wound is built around being answered and selected.

His danger is liking machine attention precisely because it asks nothing difficult of him.

## 8.8 Psychological Feedback on Roman

Roman sees his own wound externalized.

He may initially enjoy the spectacle.

Then he realizes:

> The machines are not caring about Steven. They are treating him as priority.

That may lead him to ask:

> **“Is that what I do to people?”**

The scene can turn from comedy into self-recognition.

## 8.9 Major Risk

If Roman is dysregulated, Votive Priority can become:

- possessive,
- obsessive,
- invasive,
- or aggressively protective.

A security drone may misclassify harmless proximity as threat.

A camera may refuse to look away.

A service robot may block human contact.

# 9. Maddox Conduction — Grounded Assembly

## 9.1 Core Principle

> **Maddox aligns matter through grounded intention.**

Maddox can channel integrated energy through held objects.

Steven translates this principle into physical technological coordination.

## 9.2 Working Name

> **Grounded Assembly**

Alternative terms:

- Structural Accord
- Material Alignment
- Anchor Configuration
- Integrated Formation

Grounded Assembly is the recommended term.

## 9.3 Basic Effect

Nearby technological components coordinate around a clear physical purpose.

They may:

- stabilize,
- align,
- support,
- brace,
- repair temporarily,
- or form a functional arrangement.

## 9.4 Examples

### Damaged Machines

- continue operating long enough to complete evacuation,
- realign damaged components,
- or hold a failing mechanism together temporarily.

### Maintenance Drones

- coordinate into a lifting structure,
- brace a damaged wall,
- create a path,
- or carry weight in perfect formation.

### Transit Infrastructure

- hold a jammed door,
- stabilize a rail switch,
- coordinate emergency lighting,
- or keep a failing platform mechanism functional.

### Smart Furniture and Retail Systems

- rearrange into barricades,
- create shelter,
- or clear an organized escape route.

### Robotic Arms

- move with unusually precise joint purpose,
- assemble temporary supports,
- or transform scattered materials into a usable tool.

## 9.5 Symbolic Meaning

Grounded Assembly transforms the city from a field of competing devices into a temporary community of function.

It reflects Maddox’s integrated strength:

> no spectacle, no worship, no rupture—only what is needed, held together long enough to help.

## 9.6 Psychological Feedback on Steven

Steven may feel:

- pressure to stabilize everything,
- responsibility for every failing system,
- fear of letting go,
- and the temptation to keep holding structures beyond his capacity.

This mirrors Maddox’s shadow:

> guidance becoming control.

## 9.7 Major Risk

Grounded Assembly can become over-containment.

Steven may:

- freeze a system that needs to change,
- keep broken infrastructure operating too long,
- prevent a necessary collapse,
- or treat people like components requiring proper placement.

# 10. Combined Conduction and Why It Should Be Rare

Steven may eventually be able to channel more than one field in sequence or limited combination.

This should remain rare and costly.

## 10.1 Kaiden + Maddox

Possible effect:

> Controlled rupture followed by purposeful reassembly.

Use cases:

- breaking a locked system and rebuilding a safe local pathway,
- disabling hostile automation and reconfiguring it for evacuation,
- exposing hidden network architecture and stabilizing only the necessary functions.

Risk:

- Steven begins deciding which parts of a system deserve to survive.

## 10.2 Roman + Maddox

Possible effect:

> Protective systems coordinate around a person or group.

Use cases:

- drones create a moving shield,
- public lights guide civilians,
- emergency devices prioritize the vulnerable.

Risk:

- protection becomes captivity.

## 10.3 Kaiden + Roman

Possible effect:

> Attention systems lose stable targets and become attracted to contradictory signals.

Use cases:

- confusing surveillance,
- breaking algorithmic pursuit,
- or forcing Videre’s attention architecture to expose its hidden priorities.

Risk:

- citywide spectacle,
- uncontrolled visibility,
- Feed triangulation,
- mass device instability.

## 10.4 Three-Way Conduction

This should be an endgame-level event.

Potential function:

- rupture hostile control,
- redirect attention toward what has been hidden,
- and assemble infrastructure around human survival.

Potential danger:

> Steven becomes the temporary nervous system of Alderbridge.

This cannot occur casually.

It risks:

- identity fragmentation,
- permanent network residue,
- AI entanglement,
- Feed possession,
- or Steven losing the distinction between his own will and the city’s requests.

# 11. Controlled Testing Sequence

## 11.1 Why Maddox Initiates Testing

Maddox insists on structured testing because uncontrolled discovery is too dangerous.

His goals are to determine:

- what Steven can channel,
- how consent affects the result,
- how long contact is required,
- what emotional feedback enters Steven,
- how far the effect spreads,
- which systems notice,
- and when Steven loses the ability to disengage.

Maddox does not treat the experiment like superhero training.

He treats it like accountability.

## 11.2 Recommended Location

Possible controlled locations:

- a decommissioned CivicWave testing depot,
- an abandoned delivery warehouse in Old Lantern,
- an isolated Alderline maintenance yard,
- or a temporarily disconnected Votive Commons service area.

The location should contain:

- small delivery drones,
- maintenance bots,
- disconnected cameras,
- network switches,
- Halo Lamp prototypes,
- and physical materials for Maddox testing.

It should be isolated from the live city network as much as possible.

## 11.3 Safety Conditions

Before testing:

1. Mira maps every active signal.
2. Steven disconnects the site from GroundMesh.
3. Maddox establishes a stop word.
4. Kaiden agrees not to force additional energy.
5. Roman powers down personal devices.
6. Analog monitoring is used where possible.
7. The team establishes a maximum contact duration.
8. Someone remains physically able to break contact.
9. Steven must describe what he feels before proceeding.
10. The test ends if Steven stops using first-person language.

That last rule is important.

If Steven begins saying:

- “We need…”
- “The system wants…”
- “It says…”
- “Everything is asking…”

without distinguishing himself, Maddox stops the test.

# 12. Testing Scene — Kaiden and Steven

## 12.1 Emotional Setup

Kaiden takes Steven’s wrist carefully.

Both remember their first Lattice event.

The contact contains:

- trust,
- embarrassment,
- fear,
- and unresolved emotional intimacy.

Roman notices.

## 12.2 Comedic Exchange

Roman:

> “Wow.”

Kaiden:

> “Don't.”

Roman:

> “I didn’t say anything.”

Maddox:

> “You said ‘wow’ like you were reviewing a honeymoon package.”

Roman:

> “I’m just saying, this is very *Ghost*. All we need is clay and one song neither of them will ever hear normally again.”

Steven:

> “Could you please stop narrating?”

Kaiden:

> “Thank you.”

Maddox:

> “Focus, Demi Moore.”

The joke should feel surprising because Maddox usually resists joining the teasing.

## 12.3 Ability Result

The drones begin:

- rotating,
- dropping routes,
- bumping into one another,
- reciting contradictory destinations,
- or landing simultaneously.

One repeats:

> “Recalculating.”

Then again:

> “Recalculating.”

Then:

> “Destination unavailable.”

Steven disconnects, dizzy.

Kaiden catches his arm.

Roman:

> “Oh, good. More touching. The experiment remains scientifically rigorous.”

## 12.4 Emotional Residue

The humor does not erase the concern.

Maddox notices that Kaiden’s chaos propagated much farther through Steven than expected.

Steven notices how exhilarating the branching possibilities felt.

Kaiden realizes Steven could distribute his worst impulses through the city.

Steven moves left.

The drones follow.

He moves right.

They follow.

> “But close enough that we keep testing.”

# 14. Testing Scene — Maddox and Steven

## 14.1 Tone

This test should be quieter.
